# 83(b) Election - cover letter and filing checklist

> Ready-to-print materials for the 83(b) election. Mail within 30 days of stock issuance. Missing the deadline is catastrophic and irreversible.

## What the 83(b) election does

Under default IRS treatment, you owe ordinary income tax on each vesting tranche as the stock vests. If Caventia grows to a $5M valuation by year 2, the 2M shares vesting through year 2 trigger a tax bill based on appreciated fair market value (FMV) at each vesting event. Roughly: 2M shares times your share of $5M valuation = significant taxable income with no liquidity to pay it.

The 83(b) election lets you elect to pay tax NOW on the spread between purchase price ($80) and current FMV ($80, since you just incorporated). Spread is $0. Tax is $0. From that day forward, all appreciation is treated as capital gains, taxable only on sale.

The election is free to make. Missing it is not reversible.

## The deadline

**30 days from the stock issuance date** on your stock purchase agreement. Not 30 days from Atlas signup. Not 30 days from the Delaware certificate. 30 days from the date the stock was actually issued to you.

Atlas pre-fills the date. Verify it. Mark Day 0, Day 7 and Day 28 on your calendar.

## Certified mail label

When you mail this, do it via USPS Certified Mail with Return Receipt Requested. The certified mail receipt is the proof you mailed within the 30-day window. Investors will ask for it during due diligence.

USPS Certified Mail label requirements:
- **To:** Department of the Treasury / Internal Revenue Service / Ogden, UT 84201
- **From:** Ashish K. Saxena, 16015 Cleveland Street, Apt. #611, Redmond, WA 98052
- **Service:** Certified Mail with Return Receipt (PS Form 3811 attached)
- **Approximate cost:** ~$8 first mailing, ~$8 backup mailing one week later

## Filing checklist

Day of stock issuance:
- [ ] Sign the 83(b) form Atlas pre-fills
- [ ] Print three copies: one for IRS, one for the company, one for your tax preparer
- [ ] Print the cover letter
- [ ] Print a self-addressed stamped envelope (so IRS can return your date-stamped copy)
- [ ] Put cover letter + 83(b) form + self-addressed envelope in a USPS-mailable envelope

Within 7 days of stock issuance:
- [ ] Visit USPS branch with the envelope
- [ ] Request Certified Mail with Return Receipt (PS Form 3811)
- [ ] Pay $8, get receipt
- [ ] Save the certified mail receipt in `Documents/Caventia/Corporate/83b/`

Day 7-10 after stock issuance:
- [ ] Mail a backup copy via the same Certified Mail process. Cost: $8. Insurance against the first envelope getting lost.
- [ ] Save the second receipt in the same folder

Within 60-90 days of mailing:
- [ ] Watch for the date-stamped duplicate copy returning from IRS Ogden
- [ ] Once received, scan and save in `Documents/Caventia/Corporate/83b/IRS-acknowledgement.pdf`
- [ ] Hand a copy to your tax preparer for that tax year

Permanent record:
- [ ] Tag the folder "83(b) - critical" so it surfaces in due diligence searches
- [ ] Reference the 83(b) date on your cap table notes

## What if the deadline slips

If the 30-day window passes without mailing:
- The election is forfeit. There is no extension.
- You will owe ordinary income tax on each vesting event at then-current FMV.
- The mitigation is structural: cancel the unvested portion and reissue stock at the current FMV (which by then includes any appreciation, so this is expensive).

This is why the 7-day rule exists: mail it the same week you sign Atlas paperwork. Do not wait until day 25 trusting that you remember.

## Do not delegate

Mail this yourself. Do not have an assistant, family member or anyone else handle it. The certified mail receipt has to be in your name. Investors will ask "did you personally mail the 83(b)?" and the right answer is "yes."
