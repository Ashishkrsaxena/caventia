# Animation 03 — Three lines of defense

**Placement:** `/sr117` page, immediately above the whitepaper email gate.
**Total length:** 35 seconds.
**Aspect:** 1920 × 1080.
**Audio:** Voiceover + light ambient. Three distinct fracture sounds in Act 2 (one per line). No music.
**Loop:** No. Play once on scroll-into-view, then hold the end frame.

## Intent

The three-lines-of-defense model has been the backbone of bank risk management for fifteen years. It survives, conceptually, when AI agents enter the picture. The specific workflows do not.

This animation makes that visible. It shows the traditional flow working. It shows the same flow breaking at three specific moments under AI agents. Then it shows where Caventia bridges the gap. The viewer leaves understanding why traditional MRM functions are quietly nervous about agents, and what the structural fix looks like.

Tone: respectful. The traditional framework is not wrong; it is incomplete. The animation should not feel like a takedown of MRM. It should feel like a renovation by someone who respects the original architect.

## Voiceover (read straight through)

> Three lines of defense have governed bank risk for fifteen years. First line owns the model. Second line validates it. Third line audits the whole pipeline. The framework survives the move to AI agents. The workflows do not. First-line documentation breaks when the model's behavior shifts between releases. Second-line validation breaks when reproducibility breaks. Third-line audit breaks when the production artifact is a chat transcript. Caventia is the audit-trail spine that holds the three lines together when the model under them is no longer deterministic.

## Act 1 — The framework as designed (0:00 to 0:11)

**Shot 1 (0:00 to 0:03). Three pillars rise.**

Black parchment. From below, three vertical pillars rise in sequence, 200ms stagger. Each pillar is rendered as a Roman column (subtle nod to the Caventia mark). At the top of each pillar, a label in Fraunces:

- Pillar 1: `First Line` / mono subtitle: `Model Owners`
- Pillar 2: `Second Line` / mono subtitle: `Model Risk Management`
- Pillar 3: `Third Line` / mono subtitle: `Internal Audit`

A faint hairline base joins all three pillars.

VO: "Three lines of defense have governed bank risk for fifteen years."

**Shot 2 (0:03 to 0:11). The document flows.**

A small document appears at the base of pillar 1. It is labeled `Model documentation — credit scorecard v2.3`.

The document moves to the top of pillar 1. A stamp lands: `Owned, signed`. The document moves to the top of pillar 2. A stamp lands: `Validated, signed`. The document moves to the top of pillar 3. A stamp lands: `Audited, signed`.

The document is now triple-stamped. It settles cleanly in front of the third pillar. The framework worked.

VO: "First line owns the model. Second line validates it. Third line audits the whole pipeline."

## Act 2 — The breakdown under AI agents (0:11 to 0:26)

**Shot 3 (0:11 to 0:13). The agent enters.**

The credit scorecard document at the base of pillar 1 dissolves. In its place, an agent silhouette materializes. The silhouette has the same faint shimmer used in animation 01 to signal stochasticity.

VO: "The framework survives the move to AI agents. The workflows do not."

**Shot 4 (0:13 to 0:17). First line fractures.**

The agent moves to the top of pillar 1. A model owner tries to stamp it. Their pen hovers, hesitates. A speech bubble appears beside them in body italic: *"What changed since yesterday?"*

The pillar develops a hairline crack from top to base. Fracture sound. The crack is rendered in Pompeii red.

VO: "First-line documentation breaks when the model's behavior shifts between releases."

**Shot 5 (0:17 to 0:21). Second line fractures.**

The agent moves to pillar 2. A model validator runs a reproducibility check: they input the same prompt twice. Two different outputs render. A small annotation appears: `temperature = 0, same prompt`. The two outputs still differ.

A speech bubble: *"We cannot validate a moving target."*

Second pillar fractures. Fracture sound.

VO: "Second-line validation breaks when reproducibility breaks."

**Shot 6 (0:21 to 0:26). Third line fractures.**

The agent moves to pillar 3. An auditor asks for the audit trail. The agent hands them a chat transcript: a literal back-and-forth of user and assistant lines. The auditor reads the transcript. A speech bubble: *"This is a screenshot. This is not an audit artifact."*

Third pillar fractures. Fracture sound.

All three pillars now stand with visible Pompeii red cracks running top to base.

VO: "Third-line audit breaks when the production artifact is a chat transcript."

## Act 3 — The bridge (0:26 to 0:35)

**Shot 7 (0:26 to 0:30). Caventia spine arrives.**

A horizontal spine slides in beneath the three pillars, from left to right. The spine is rendered as a thick deep-ink line with the Pompeii red shaft visible at its center (consistent with the brand sigil). Above the spine, in mono uppercase tracked 0.18em: `Caventia · audit-trail spine`.

As the spine touches each pillar's base, the Pompeii red crack stops growing and begins to heal upward.

VO: "Caventia is the audit-trail spine..."

**Shot 8 (0:30 to 0:34). Each line gets what it needs.**

Three artifacts emerge from the spine, one to each pillar.

- To pillar 1: a card titled `Behavioral envelope`. Subtitle in mono: `for first-line documentation`.
- To pillar 2: a card titled `Tool-call trace`. Subtitle: `for second-line validation`.
- To pillar 3: a card titled `Version-pinned attestation`. Subtitle: `for third-line audit`.

Each card slots in at the top of its pillar. The cracks finish healing. The pillars stand whole again, this time with a small Pompeii red base stripe connecting each one back to the spine.

VO: "...that holds the three lines together when the model under them is no longer deterministic."

**Shot 9 (0:34 to 0:35). End frame.**

The three pillars, the spine beneath them, the three artifacts at the top. Below the whole composition, in mono uppercase: `Three lines, held together by one spine.`

To the right: the Caventia sigil.

Hold on this frame. Do not loop.
